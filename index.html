A long time ago, in a server room far, far away…

 

The cloud empires grew powerful. AWS, Azure, GCP — their outages rippled across the galaxy, taking down startups, banks, and the occasional smart fridge.

 

But on a desk in Hamburg, a small device hummed quietly.

 

8 gigabytes of RAM. An NVMe SSD. A Hailo-10H neural accelerator capable of 40 trillion operations per second.

 

It asked for no subscription. It sent no data to the mothership. It simply… ran.

 

You are its last line of defence.

 

Incoming: a cascading Lambda timeout. A rogue S3 bucket policy. A DynamoDB throttle event.

 

Defend the Pi. Defend local compute. Defend the right to run your own stack.

 

May the uptime be with you.
